I'm Marco Verón, a security engineer based in Asunción, Paraguay. I work at a cybersecurity company, where I research, design and build tools and automations for SOC and CSIRT teams. Before that I worked as a SOC analyst, monitoring alerts, supporting incident response and automating detection and response with Wazuh and Python.

I'm in the final year of a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering at Universidad Politécnica Taiwán-Paraguay, a program taught entirely in English, and spent 2025–2026 on exchange at the National Taiwan University of Science and Technology in Taipei.

## What I work on

- **LLM agents for security operations.** Tool-using agents that work over SIEM data and map what they see to MITRE ATT&CK.
- **Guardrails for those agents.** Human-in-the-loop approval, dry-run modes and append-only audit trails. An agent that can block an IP needs a gate it cannot skip.
- **SOC automation.** Triage workflows, detection logic with Sigma and YARA, and the Python that ties it together.

Lately I'm studying how to attack and secure LLM applications: prompt injection, excessive agency and the OWASP Top 10 for LLMs.
